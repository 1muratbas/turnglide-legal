Turnglide Educational Disclaimer

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Turnglide is an independent educational application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the California DMV or any government agency.

A study aid, not the test

  • The questions, lessons, lesson diagrams and road sign illustrations are original material written from the California Driver's Handbook (California DMV).
  • They are not questions from any DMV test, and Turnglide does not know which questions appear on any DMV test.
  • Each question was checked in a separate automated review pass against the handbook section it cites; no human driving-education expert has reviewed it yet. The 10 lesson diagrams were checked against their cited sections. The content has not been reviewed or approved by the California DMV or by a licensed driving instructor.

The law is the authority

Traffic laws change. The California Vehicle Code and current California DMV publications are the authority; the handbook notes that it is a summary of the law. Check https://www.dmv.ca.gov for current requirements.

Scores are practice only

  • Practice exam scores and the Study Readiness summary describe your practice in this app only. They do not predict or guarantee your result on any test.
  • Study Readiness is a simple rule-based summary of your coverage and accuracy. It is not a probability of passing.
  • Practice exams are graded at 80%, the passing score the California DMV publishes. Exam lengths (20 questions for Quick Check, 40 for full practice exams) and the optional timers are app choices, not the DMV test format.

Coverage

California Class C only. Other states are not available.

Not legal advice

Nothing in the app is legal advice. When driving, always obey posted signs, signals and law enforcement.
