How to Delete Your Turnglide Data

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Turnglide is an independent educational application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the California DMV or any government agency.

Turnglide has no account and stores your data only on your iPhone, so you can delete it yourself at any time.

Delete one question report

1. Open Settings › Feedback › My Question Reports.

2. Swipe left on the report and tap Delete.

Delete all question reports

1. Open Settings › Feedback › My Question Reports.

2. Tap Delete All Reports, then Delete All.

Delete everything in the app

1. Open Settings and scroll down.

2. Tap Reset All Progress, then Erase Everything.

This deletes your answers, bookmarks, exams, streak, settings, first name, question reports and scheduled reminders on this iPhone. It cannot be undone. Purchases are not affected and can be restored with Restore Purchases.

Delete the app

Press and hold the app icon › Remove App › Delete App. This removes all app data from the iPhone, including any damaged files the app had set aside. Copies in older iPhone backups remain until those backups are replaced or deleted.

Exported files

If you exported reports as JSON or CSV, delete those files from the Files app (or wherever you saved them). The app cannot delete them for you.

Subscriptions

Deleting data or the app does not cancel a subscription. Cancel in iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, or in the app under Settings › Manage Subscription (shown while Premium is active).
