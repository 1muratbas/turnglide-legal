Options

OptionTypeBilling
Premium WeeklyAuto-renewable subscriptionEvery week, until canceled

Weekly and Monthly are in one subscription group, so you can hold only one of them at a time. Prices are shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy, in your local currency.

What Premium includes

Unlimited daily practice and the full question bank · 40-question practice exams (with an optional timed version) · Smart Review (spaced repetition) · My Weak Spots analysis · the full 7-day study plan · detailed progress statistics · every road sign in the trainer.

Always free: every lesson (including the lesson diagrams), 20 practice questions a day (up to 10 per topic), the 20-question Quick Check exam, 12 road signs, links to California DMV resources, and Report a Question.

Premium covers California Class C content only.

Payment and renewal

  • Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase.
  • A subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • If the price changes, Apple notifies you as its rules require, and may ask for your consent.

Free trials and offers

No free trial or introductory offer is configured at this time. If one is offered later, the app will show its length and the price that applies after it ends before you start it.

Managing and canceling

  • Cancel or change plans in iPhone Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions, or in the app Settings › Manage Subscription (shown while Premium is active), or from Manage subscriptions on the Premium screen.
  • Canceling stops the next renewal. Premium stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Restoring

Use Restore Purchases (Premium screen or Settings) on any iPhone signed in with the same Apple ID.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple: https://reportaproblem.apple.com. We cannot issue refunds directly.

Offline use

The app remembers your Premium status on the device so it works offline, and re-checks it with Apple when it can.

Changes to Premium

If we change Premium in a way that materially reduces what current subscribers receive, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
