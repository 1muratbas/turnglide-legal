Common questions

Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-in. Your progress stays on your iPhone.

Does it work offline? Yes. Lessons, questions, diagrams and signs are built in. You need a connection only to buy or restore Premium and to open DMV website links.

Which states are supported? California Class C only.

Are these the DMV's test questions? No. They are original practice questions written from the California Vehicle Code and checked against the current statute text in two separate automated AI review passes. They have not been reviewed by the DMV, a lawyer or a licensed driving instructor.

Does a high score mean I will pass? No. Practice scores and Study Readiness describe your practice in the app only. Check https://www.dmv.ca.gov for current requirements.

Is the app accessible? It is built for VoiceOver (a spoken description for every lesson diagram and the spoken name of every road sign), Dynamic Type, Dark Mode and Reduce Motion. Text inside sign and diagram artwork does not resize; each drawing has a text description. Tell us about any barrier at 1muratbas@gmail.com.

Report a question you think is wrong

1. Under the question or its explanation, tap Report a problem.

2. Pick a reason, optionally add a comment, and tap Save. The report is saved only on your iPhone; it is not sent to us.

3. To send it to us: Settings › Feedback › My Question Reports › Export as JSON (or CSV), save the file, and attach it to an email to 1muratbas@gmail.com. Please don't include personal information.
