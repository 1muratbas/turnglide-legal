Turnglide Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Applies to: Turnglide for iPhone, version 1.2.0 and later, until this policy is updated.

Turnglide is an independent educational application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the California DMV or any government agency.

1. Who we are

Turnglide is published by Murat Baş, Kırşehir Ahi Evran Üniversitesi, Eğitim Fakültesi, 3. Kat, Kırşehir, Türkiye ("we", "us"). Questions about this policy: 1muratbas@gmail.com. Support page: https://1muratbas.github.io/turnglide-legal/.

2. Summary

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • We do not collect personal information through the app. Your study data and your question reports are stored only on your device.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and does not use the advertising identifier.
  • Nothing you save in the app is sent to us automatically. We have no servers for the app.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves the following in its private storage on your iPhone (the app's Application Support folder). It is not sent to us.

4. "Report a Question"

Under practice questions and explanations you can tap Report a problem to save a note about a question you think is wrong.

  • What a report contains: a random report ID, the question ID, the content version, the state (for example, CA), the reason you chose, an optional comment you type (up to 1,000 characters), and the date and time.
  • Where it is kept: only on your device, in the app's private storage.
  • Nothing is sent automatically. This version of the app has no way to send reports to us or anyone else.
  • View and delete: Settings › Feedback › My Question Reports. You can delete one report, or all of them. Reset All Progress also deletes all reports.
  • Export: you can export your reports as a JSON or CSV file. The app opens the iOS save dialog, and the file is written only where you choose (for example, On My iPhone or iCloud Drive). If you then decide to email the file to us, we receive what you send (see section 8).
  • Please do not put personal information in the comment.

5. Analytics, advertising and tracking

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or attribution software. It does not use the advertising identifier, does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and does not sell or share personal information. Progress statistics in the app are calculated on your device.

6. Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. The app uses Apple's StoreKit on your device to load prices, make purchases, restore them and check your Premium status. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple handles this information under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

7. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, iOS schedules them locally on your device. The app does not use push notifications. You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings.

8. Contacting us

If you email us at 1muratbas@gmail.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include, such as an exported report file. We use it only to reply and to correct content. We keep support emails for 12 months, unless you ask us to delete them sooner. See the Data Retention Policy.

9. Crash data provided by Apple

The app has no crash-reporting software. If you choose in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements to share data with app developers, Apple may provide us with crash reports and performance data that Apple collected. Apple does not identify you to us in this data.

10. Links to other websites

Links to the California DMV website and to Apple pages open in your browser. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

11. Children and teens

Turnglide is meant for learner drivers, including teens. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, contact 1muratbas@gmail.com and we will delete it.

12. Your choices and rights

  • Delete data on your device: see the Data Deletion Instructions (Settings › Reset All Progress, deleting individual reports, or deleting the app).
  • Requests to us: because we hold no app data, a request to access or delete data usually concerns only support emails. Send it to 1muratbas@gmail.com. Privacy laws where you live, such as California law, may give you additional rights.

13. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, protected by iOS. Exported files are under your control once you save them.

14. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy, the effective date, the in-app copy and the App Store privacy information before any change takes effect. Examples: sending question reports to us, adding analytics, crash reporting, accounts or sync.
