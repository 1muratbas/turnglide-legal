How the content was made

  • 544 practice questions, 17 lessons, 20 road sign descriptions and 9 lesson diagram captions were written from the text of the Vehicle Code sections they cite. The California Driver's Handbook and the earlier version of this app's question bank were not used as sources for this version.
  • Every question keeps a record of its Vehicle Code sections, a short quote of the operative wording, the date the statute text was checked, and the results of its checks.
  • Each question was checked against the current statute text in two separate automated AI review passes. No human expert, lawyer or driving instructor has reviewed the content. Questions that failed a pass were rewritten or replaced and checked again.
  • A text-similarity screen against the DMV handbook was also run as a safety check. It is a screening signal only and is not proof of independence or a legal opinion.
  • Some practical tips in lessons are labelled "Good practice (not a legal requirement)".
  • Statutes change. The Vehicle Code on leginfo.legislature.ca.gov is the authority.

Lesson diagrams

Nine original vector drawings, drawn in code (SwiftUI Canvas). No DMV, Caltrans or third-party artwork was copied. Each shows facts tied to Vehicle Code sections, has a VoiceOver description and links to its source. Where a drawing shows a common practical technique that the statute does not state (such as wheel direction when parking on a hill or the three-second gap), the caption says so.
